# Anamika Dristi, Ph.D.

Environmental scientist working on water quality, watershed hydrology and carbon biogeochemistry in rivers, lakes and estuaries. Ph.D. in Renewable Natural Resources, Louisiana State University (2026). B.S. in Fisheries.

**Tools:** R, Python, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, statistics (mixed models, GAMs, time series, meta-analysis), remote sensing (Landsat, Sentinel, MODIS, GRACE), field and laboratory water quality methods.

## Projects

These are independent analyses of public data. Each repository has its data sources, methods, results and limitations in its README.

### Meta-analysis
- [soil-health-regenerative-meta-analysis](https://github.com/anamikadristi/soil-health-regenerative-meta-analysis): Three-level random-effects meta-analysis in R (metafor) of conservation and regenerative practices and soil health indicators, using the public global Soil Health Database.

### Environmental health
- [drinking-water-violations-tx-la](https://github.com/anamikadristi/drinking-water-violations-tx-la): Health-based drinking water violations at Texas and Louisiana community water systems (EPA SDWIS, 2015-2025) linked to system size, source water and county social vulnerability.
- [houston-urban-heat-social-vulnerability](https://github.com/anamikadristi/houston-urban-heat-social-vulnerability): Landsat 8/9 summer land surface temperature and NDVI for Harris County, Texas, linked to CDC/ATSDR SVI and land cover with spatial regression and hot spot analysis.
- [industrial-emissions-proximity-houston-baton-rouge](https://github.com/anamikadristi/industrial-emissions-proximity-houston-baton-rouge): Residential proximity to EPA TRI air-release facilities in Harris County and the Louisiana Mississippi River corridor, with buffer analysis, an emissions index and Gi* hot spots.
- [texas-groundwater-arsenic-nitrate-geostatistics](https://github.com/anamikadristi/texas-groundwater-arsenic-nitrate-geostatistics): Spatial statistics and indicator kriging of arsenic and nitrate exceedance of EPA limits in the Gulf Coast and Ogallala aquifers (TWDB well data).

### Modeling
- [mississippi-carbon-trends-ml-boxmodel](https://github.com/anamikadristi/mississippi-carbon-trends-ml-boxmodel): Lower Mississippi River carbon and water chemistry from public USGS data: trend tests, machine learning, pCO2 estimates and a Monte Carlo calibrated box model.
- [river-carbon-gam-mixing-uncertainty](https://github.com/anamikadristi/river-carbon-gam-mixing-uncertainty): GAMs, concentration-discharge behaviour, flow regimes and a two end-member mixing model with Monte Carlo uncertainty for the Mississippi-Atchafalaya system.
- [gulf-hypoxia-nutrient-load-model](https://github.com/anamikadristi/gulf-hypoxia-nutrient-load-model): Regression and Bayesian models linking May Mississippi-Atchafalaya nutrient loads to the Gulf hypoxic zone area, 1985-2026, with a load reduction estimate.

### Machine learning and AI
- [dissolved-oxygen-forecasting-machine-learning](https://github.com/anamikadristi/dissolved-oxygen-forecasting-machine-learning): Forecasting daily minimum dissolved oxygen and hypoxia risk at USGS monitors in Louisiana and Texas with random forest, LightGBM and an LSTM, interpreted with SHAP.
- [coastal-wetland-landcover-deep-learning](https://github.com/anamikadristi/coastal-wetland-landcover-deep-learning): Deep learning (CNN and U-Net) classification of coastal Louisiana wetland land cover from Sentinel-2 imagery, with a random forest baseline and change mapping.

### GIS and Google Earth
- [amite-watershed-wetland-gis](https://github.com/anamikadristi/amite-watershed-wetland-gis): Land cover change, wetlands and water quality in the Amite, Tickfaw and Lake Maurepas basins using NLCD, NWI, NHDPlus and Water Quality Portal data.
- [dissertation-study-areas-gis-mapping](https://github.com/anamikadristi/dissertation-study-areas-gis-mapping): Study-area maps, an ArcGIS Pro ready geodatabase and a Google Earth KMZ for three published Louisiana river and wastewater carbon studies.
- [flood-hazard-exposure-gis-baton-rouge](https://github.com/anamikadristi/flood-hazard-exposure-gis-baton-rouge): FEMA flood zone exposure of population, housing and critical facilities in East Baton Rouge and Livingston Parishes, with SVI overlay and an ArcGIS Pro workflow.
- [louisiana-coastal-wetlands-google-earth](https://github.com/anamikadristi/louisiana-coastal-wetlands-google-earth): CRMS salinity and water level trends, USGS land loss by basin and a Google Earth KMZ tour of Louisiana coastal monitoring stations.

### Remote sensing
- [tallgrass-prairie-fire-grazing-remote-sensing](https://github.com/anamikadristi/tallgrass-prairie-fire-grazing-remote-sensing): MODIS NDVI and EVI response to fire and grazing at Konza Prairie with mixed models and GAMs, plus Google Earth Engine scripts for a Landsat extension.
- [lake-pontchartrain-sentinel2-water-quality](https://github.com/anamikadristi/lake-pontchartrain-sentinel2-water-quality): Sentinel-2 turbidity, chlorophyll and CDOM proxies for Lake Pontchartrain and Lake Maurepas, 2019-2024, with Hurricane Ida and Bonnet Carre Spillway events.
- [sentinel1-sar-flood-mapping-harvey](https://github.com/anamikadristi/sentinel1-sar-flood-mapping-harvey): Hurricane Harvey flood extent from Sentinel-1 SAR with thresholding, change detection and HAND masking, validated against USGS data, with population exposure.
- [texas-drought-vegetation-modis-et](https://github.com/anamikadristi/texas-drought-vegetation-modis-et): MODIS vegetation, evapotranspiration and land surface temperature anomalies during the 2011 and 2022 Texas droughts by ecoregion, compared with the US Drought Monitor.

### Groundwater
- [grace-groundwater-storage-texas-aquifers](https://github.com/anamikadristi/grace-groundwater-storage-texas-aquifers): Groundwater storage change in Texas major aquifers from GRACE and GRACE-FO, 2002-2026, checked against TWDB well water levels.
- [houston-groundwater-levels-land-subsidence](https://github.com/anamikadristi/houston-groundwater-levels-land-subsidence): USGS groundwater levels and GNSS land subsidence in the Houston-Galveston region, with kriged subsidence rates and links to aquifer decline.
- [baton-rouge-aquifer-saltwater-intrusion](https://github.com/anamikadristi/baton-rouge-aquifer-saltwater-intrusion): Water-level trends, breakpoints and chloride encroachment across the Baton Rouge fault in the Southern Hills aquifer system.
